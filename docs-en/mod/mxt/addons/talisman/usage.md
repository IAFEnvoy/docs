---
title: Using the Drawing Table
---

Place `mxt_talisman:talisman_workstation` and put paper accepted by a drawing recipe in its paper slot. Prepare a core brush and pigment; refill by holding the brush on the cursor and clicking pigment in any container. Choose a formula in the table, trace its reference strokes with the brush, then submit. The recipe and scoring rules determine consumption, completion and output quality.

See the [drawing recipe tutorial](./tutorial/author-a-talisman-drawing.md), [scoring rules](./technical/talisman-scoring.md) and [brush pigment](../../player-guide/brush-and-pigment.md).
