MITE: Reforged rebuilds the demanding survival loop of MITE 1.6.4 on **Minecraft 1.21.1 + NeoForge 21.1**. It is not merely a content mod that adds ores and monsters. Health, hunger, nutrition, harvesting requirements, timed crafting, material tiers, farming, livestock production, moon phases, the Underworld, and monster behavior form one interconnected ruleset.

:::warning Survive by MITE's rules

New players start with only 3 hearts and 3 hunger icons by default. Mining bare-handed, skipping workbench tiers, or living on a single food source are not reliable strategies. Read the [progression guide](./getting-started/progression.md) before entering your first world.

:::

## Current content

- Tiered workbenches, heat-based furnaces, material anvils, metal buckets, additional weapons, and equipment quality.
- Protein, essential fats, phytonutrients, and insulin response, with a dedicated nutrition screen and HUD.
- Onions, blueberries, fertilized farmland, crop disease, and livestock with food, water, and freedom needs.
- Silver, ancient metal, mithril, and adamantium material chains; copper uses vanilla Minecraft resources.
- The Underworld, a three-stage standard portal route, 16 rune patterns, and long-distance same-dimension rune gates.
- MITE undead, spiders, bats, gelatinous mobs, elementals, and server-authoritative AI.
- Optional JEI, EMI, and Jade integration.
